Mining Discovery

Mining Discovery is a global digital platform and event organizer connecting investors, explorers, and industry innovators. It offers project-matching tools, digital branding solutions, and networking opportunities to help early-stage mining ventures attract investment and build visibility through curated showcases and strategic partnerships.

U.S. Gold Corp

U.S. Gold Corp specializes in gold exploration and development across the United States, including the flagship CK Gold Project in Wyoming. CEO George Bee leads a team with extensive mine-building experience, focusing on permitting milestones and near-term production. The company also develops the Keystone project in Nevada and the Challis project in Idaho.

Power Metallic

Power Metallic is a Canadian exploration company focused on developing the high-grade Nickel-Copper-PGM-Gold-Silver Nisk project in Quebec. With a strong technical foundation and a commitment to sustainable exploration, the company aims to advance the Nisk project toward becoming Canada’s next major polymetallic mine.

Kodiak Copper

Kodiak Copper Corp is a Canadian exploration company developing high-value copper-gold projects. Focused on its MPD project in British Columbia, the company combines innovation, sustainability, and strong leadership to unlock resource potential and support the growing demand for copper in a clean-energy and technology-driven future.

Aurion Resources

Aurion Resources Ltd (TSX-V: AU, OTCQX: AIRRF) is a well-funded Canadian exploration company focused on early-stage precious metals opportunities. The company advances projects through in-house exploration and joint ventures with partners such as B2Gold, Kinross Gold, and KoBold Metals in Finland. Aurion’s flagship project is the wholly owned Risti gold project.

Phenom Resources

Phenom Resources Ltd focuses on gold and vanadium exploration in Nevada, USA. Led by a highly experienced technical team, the company holds patented processes for extracting vanadium and nickel. With its Carlin-type gold targets and one of America’s largest strategic vanadium resources, Phenom is well positioned to serve both the precious metals and clean-energy markets.

Noble Plains Uranium Corp.

Noble Plains Uranium Corp is a U.S.-based uranium exploration company developing ISR-compatible projects across key states such as Wyoming. The company upgrades historic uranium assets and rapidly advances NI 43-101 compliant resources, aiming to meet growing domestic uranium demand through capital-efficient and environmentally responsible In-Situ Recovery operations.

Astra Exploration Inc.

Astra Exploration Inc is a U.S.-based uranium exploration company focused on developing ISR-compatible projects in key regions such as Wyoming. The company advances historic uranium assets to compliant NI 43-101 resources, aiming to meet the growing domestic uranium demand through capital-efficient and environmentally responsible In-Situ Recovery operations.

Loyalist Exploration Ltd.

Loyalist Exploration Ltd. is a U.S.-based uranium exploration company advancing ISR-compatible projects across Wyoming and other key regions. The company focuses on upgrading historic uranium assets into compliant NI 43-101 resources while emphasizing environmentally friendly and cost-efficient In-Situ Recovery operations to meet the nation’s growing uranium demand.

Guanajuato Silver Company Ltd.

Guanajuato Silver Company Ltd. focuses on reactivating precious metal mines in Mexico’s historically rich Guanajuato region. The company operates five mines and three production facilities, with the El Cubo Mine serving as the core of its hub-and-spoke strategy. Managed by an experienced Mexican leadership team, it continues to expand silver and gold production in one of the world’s oldest mining districts.

Digipower X

Digipower X operates multiple advanced energy sites, including state-of-the-art combined cycle systems and high-capacity substations. The company enhances and stabilizes the energy grid, serving industrial clients and supporting global power demands with innovative energy infrastructure solutions.

Arras Minerals Corp.

Arras Minerals Corp. is a Canadian exploration company focused on copper and gold assets in northeastern Kazakhstan. Holding one of the country’s largest exploration license portfolios, the company recently entered a strategic alliance with Teck Resources to fund a US$5 million generative exploration program. Ongoing work includes IP surveys, soil sampling, and drilling across the Elemes and Tay properties.

Gold Hunter Resources.

Gold Hunter Resources is advancing high-potential gold and base metal projects through focused exploration and strong technical expertise. With a commitment to discovery and sustainable growth, the company continues to unlock value across its strategic assets, driving opportunities in emerging mineral-rich regions.

BluEnergies.

Blu Energies is an independent exploration company advancing oil and gas discoveries across frontier basins. With a focus on innovation, sustainability, and data-driven insights, Blu Energies leverages advanced seismic technologies to unlock new energy opportunities and strengthen global resource development.
