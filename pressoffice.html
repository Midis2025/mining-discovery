Mining Discovery

As a digital platform and event organizer, Mining Discovery connects investors, explorers, and industry innovators. It provides tools for project matching, digital branding, and global networking—enabling early-stage mining ventures to reach investors and build momentum through curated showcases and partnerships.

U.S Gold Corp

Specializes in gold exploration and development across the United States, including the flagship CK Gold Project in Wyoming. CEO George Bee leads a team with extensive mine-building experience, and the company aims for permitting milestones and near-term production. Further projects include Keystone in Nevada and Challis in Idaho.

Aurion Resources

Aurion Resources Ltd. (Aurion), is a well-funded, Canadian exploration company listed on the TSX Venture Exchange (TSX-V:AU) and the OTCQX Best Market (OTCQX:AIRRF). Aurion’s strategy is to generate or acquire early stage precious metals exploration opportunities and advance them through direct exploration by our experienced team or through business partnerships and joint venture arrangements. Aurion’s current focus is exploring on its wholly owned Risti project, as well as advancing joint venture arrangements with B2Gold Corp., Kinross Gold Corporation, and KoBold Metals Company in Finland.

Phenom Resources

Focused on gold and vanadium in Nevada, Phenom is led by a seasoned technical team with decades of combined experience and three patented processes for extracting vanadium and nickel. Their portfolio includes Carlin‑type gold targets alongside America’s largest strategic vanadium resource, positioning the company to capitalize on both precious metals and green-energy markets.

Noble Plains Uranium Corp.

A U.S.-based uranium exploration company developing ISR-compatible projects in states like Wyoming. Noble Plains upgrades historic uranium assets and quickly advances compliant NI 43‑101 resources. They aim to meet rising domestic uranium demand through capital-efficient, environmentally friendly In Situ Recovery operations.

Guanajuato Silver Company Ltd.

Focused on reactivating precious metal mines in Mexico’s historically rich Guanajuato region, the company owns five mines and three production facilities. Its El Cubo Mine serves as the hub of a hub‑and‑spoke strategy centered in one of the world’s oldest silver camps. Operated by a fully Mexican management team.

Digipower X

Arras Minerals Corp.

A Canadian explorer focused on copper‑gold assets in northeastern Kazakhstan. Holding the third-largest license package in the country, Arras recently formed a alliance with Teck Resources, under which Teck will sole-fund a US$5 million generative exploration program. The company is undergoing extensive data collection (IP surveys, soil sampling, drilling) across the Elemes and Tay properties.
