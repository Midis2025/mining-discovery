About

George Bee

President and CEO of U.S. Gold Corp.

U.S. Gold Corp. (NASDAQ: USAU) underwent a major shift with the appointment of George Bee as President and CEO in August 2020. Bringing over four decades of mine-building expertise, Bee redirected the company’s focus from speculative exploration to disciplined development, particularly advancing the Copper King project.

Mining Discovery provides daily mining news, a weekly mining newsletter, a monthly mining magazine and an interactive mining website for mining companies, mining associations and mining executives globally.
