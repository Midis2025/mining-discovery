CEO Profile

George Bee

President and CEO of U.S. Gold Corp.

U.S. Gold Corp. (NASDAQ: USAU) underwent a major shift with the appointment of George Bee as President and CEO in August 2020. Bringing over four decades of mine-building expertise, Bee redirected the company’s focus from speculative exploration to disciplined development, particularly advancing the Copper King project. His pragmatic, results-driven approach instilled stakeholder confidence and positioned the company on a clear path toward production.
